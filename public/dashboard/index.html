    Total transactions
    Every payment attempt in the date range, any status: COUNT(*).
    Gross transaction value (GTV)
    Value of successful payments before refunds: SUM(amount) WHERE status = 'success'. Failed, pending and cancelled attempts are excluded because no money moved.
    Refund amount
    Sum of valid refunds against successful payments made in the range. Refunds are attributed to the original payment date, so they can never exceed that period's GTV.
    Net transaction value
    GTV − refund amount. This is money kept by merchants, not our revenue.
    Fee revenue
    Processing fees the platform earns on successful payments: SUM(fee_amount) WHERE status = 'success'. UPI is zero-fee.
    Net revenue
    Fee revenue − fees reversed on refunds, assuming fees are reversed pro-rata: fee × refunded ÷ amount.
    Refund rate
    Refund amount ÷ GTV (by value). Also shown: refunded payments ÷ successful payments (by count).
    Average transaction value (ATV)
    GTV ÷ number of successful payments.
    Success rate
    Successful ÷ (successful + failed). Pending and cancelled are left out because they have no final outcome or were abandoned by the user.
    Dates
    Stored as UTC timestamptz; days and months are grouped in India Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata). Both ends of the range are inclusive.
